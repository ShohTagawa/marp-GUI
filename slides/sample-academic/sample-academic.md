<div class="page-title">A normal week</div>

## Each week has two fixed slots

<div class="sections">
<div class="sec-box">

### Monday: plan

- Choose this week's three goals
- Block the hours in your calendar

</div>
<div class="sec-box">

### Friday: review

- Tick off what is done
- Move what is left to next week

</div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">Fifteen minutes on Monday and on Friday keep the week on track</div>

---

<!-- _class: split -->

<div class="page-title">Two ways</div>

## Plan by hours, or plan by tasks

<div class="split-body">
<div class="left">

### Fixed hours

- Study at set times every weekday
- Easy to keep as a habit
- Easy to drift off task

</div>
<div class="right">

### Fixed tasks

- Finish one set task each day
- A clear end point
- Hard days run late

</div>
</div>

<div class="takeaway">Mix both: fixed hours for routine, fixed tasks for deadlines</div>

<div class="page-title">Questions</div>

## Questions students often ask

<div class="qa-grid">
<div>Q. I missed a whole week. Start again?</div>
<div>A. No. Move the missed goals into the next two weeks.</div>
<div>Q. How many goals per week?</div>
<div>A. Three is enough; one large and two small.</div>
<div>Q. Paper or app?</div>
<div>A. Either. Use the one you will open every day.</div>
</div>

<!-- _class: wrap -->

<div class="page-title">Wrap-up</div>

## What to take away

- Map the term in three phases before it starts
- Plan on Monday, review on Friday
- When a plan slips, move goals instead of starting over
